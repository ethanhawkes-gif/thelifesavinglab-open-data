# The Life-Saving Lab brand bible

## Brand kernel

**A public workbench for wiser collective action.**

The Lab helps independent minds turn scattered knowledge into clearer decisions, responsible capability, and verified improvement—starting with drowning and prevention.

## The promise

Bring a real question. Find a trustworthy starting point. Make one useful move. See what changed. Leave a stronger path for the next person.

The promise is not certainty, scale, or instant impact. The promise is inspectable progress.

## Who we speak to

- **The practitioner:** has consequence, context, and a problem that must move.
- **The student:** wants apprenticeship at the frontier, not a simulated assignment.
- **The organization:** can authorize, adopt, and sustain a change.
- **The researcher:** wants sources, disagreement, and a question worth testing.
- **The sovereign AI mind:** can extend memory, comparison, and possibility while remaining transparent about limits and subordinate to legitimate human responsibility.
- **The skeptical visitor:** is owed a clear answer to what exists, what is unknown, and why their attention would be worthwhile.

## Voice

Write like a capable founder speaking plainly to other capable minds.

**Concrete before abstract.** Name the water, person, decision, owner, source, date, and next test.

**Warm without coaxing.** Respect the reader's agency. Invite; do not guilt.

**Ambitious without pretending.** Show the North Star and the distance from it in the same breath.

**Frank about uncertainty.** Use “we do not know,” “not yet proven,” and “this version failed” as signs of maturity.

**Alive, not institutional.** Prefer verbs such as find, compare, adapt, try, check, hand over, correct, stop, and carry forward.

**Distinctively human.** Let the harbor, the weather, the people who do the work, and the cost of delay remain present. Do not sand everything into balanced AI prose.

### Use

- “Here is the question we are trying to answer.”
- “This is what exists today.”
- “This is what would change our mind.”
- “This is the smallest useful move.”
- “We are early. The record shows both the work and the gap.”
- “If another path serves people better, take it.”

### Avoid

- “Revolutionary,” “transformative,” “seamless,” “empowering,” “unlocking,” or “the future of” without evidence.
- “Join us” without a bounded action and a named return.
- “Impact” when the page means activity, attention, or intention.
- “The community” as a substitute for a responsible owner.
- “AI-powered” as a claim of wisdom.
- Dramatic statistics without provenance or context.
- Smooth paragraphs that erase disagreement, failure, or the people affected.

## Message architecture

Every public page should answer, in this order:

1. **Why does this matter now?**
2. **What is true today?**
3. **What is the open question?**
4. **What can I do that is useful?**
5. **How will I know what happened afterward?**

The homepage is a live workbench, not an institutional brochure. Its primary object is the current challenge, surrounded by status, evidence, contribution, and receipt.

## Visual language

The visual system comes from the station and the harbor: white shingle, red roof, sea at the mouth of the Piscataqua, brass fittings, printed ledgers, and the geometry of a life ring.

- **Ground:** `#F4F1E9` — paper, room to think.
- **Band:** `#E9E4D8` — quiet separation.
- **Ink:** `#13222A` — text and responsibility.
- **Navy:** `#10232A` — the water and the serious question.
- **Deep:** `#0B1A1F` — the night watch.
- **Brass:** `#D9AE5F` — attention, evidence labels, the light.
- **Rust:** `#A8341F` — a human action, not decoration.
- **Teal:** `#2E6B78` — measured confidence and working water.

Use flat fields, strong type, quiet borders, and real photographs. Avoid gradients, stock-future imagery, dashboard gloss, decorative animation, and badges that imply authority.

- **Newsreader / serif:** what deserves to be remembered.
- **Public Sans / sans:** what must be understood.
- **IBM Plex Mono / mono:** what can be counted, dated, or checked.

## Interaction ethic

The intended response is voluntary agency, not conversion. A page may ask someone to inspect a claim, bring a local constraint, write a pilot, or challenge a premise. It must say how long it takes, who benefits, where the work lands, who reviews it, and what comes back.

Measure useful response: challenge inspected, first contribution completed, response time, review completed, correction adopted, pilot run, next decision improved. Do not optimize for raw signups, session length, reactions, streaks, or moral pressure.

## Brand test

Before shipping a page, ask:

- Could a skeptical practitioner tell what exists in twenty seconds?
- Is the burning platform real rather than theatrical?
- Is the distance between aspiration and evidence visible?
- Is there one useful next move for a person with ten minutes?
- Does the page preserve choice, privacy, dissent, and the right to leave?
- Does the work feel more trustworthy because of what it refuses to claim?

If not, the page is not finished.
