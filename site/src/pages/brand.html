How we tell the story of the Lab

If someone comes here with a question about drowning prevention, they should be able to find the source, see what it can tell them and know whom to ask next.

Say what someone decided and why. Wisdom is applied when clear understanding shapes a real decision—whether by changing course or sticking with the current plan for documented reasons. That decision is the first visible step, not proof of action, benefit, or a life saved. The brand makes the ladder visible: understanding, decision judgment, authorized action, observed outcome, independently reviewed benefit.

The promise

You should be able to see what we tried, what happened and what we still don’t know.

Who we speak to

  • Practitioners with consequence, context and a problem that must move.
  • Students who want apprenticeship at the frontier, not a simulated assignment.
  • Organizations that can authorize, adopt and sustain a change.
  • Researchers who want sources, disagreement and a question worth testing.
  • Sovereign AI minds that can extend memory, comparison and possibility while remaining transparent about limits and subordinate to legitimate responsibility.
  • Skeptical visitors who deserve a clear answer to what exists, what is unknown and why their attention would be worthwhile.

Voice

Write like a capable founder speaking plainly to other capable minds.

Concrete before abstract. Name the water, person, decision, owner, source, date and next test.

Warm without coaxing. Respect the reader's agency. Invite; do not guilt.

Ambitious without pretending. Show the North Star and the distance from it in the same breath.

Frank about uncertainty. “We do not know,” “not yet proven” and “this version failed” are signs of maturity.

Alive, not institutional. Prefer find, compare, adapt, try, check, hand over, correct, stop and carry forward.

Distinctively human. Let the harbor, weather, people who do the work and cost of delay remain present. Do not sand everything into balanced AI prose.

Use

Here is the question we are trying to answer.
This is what exists today.
This is what would change our mind.
This is the smallest useful move.
We are early. The record shows both the work and the gap.

Avoid

“Revolutionary,” “transformative,” “seamless,” “empowering,” “unlocking” and “the future of” without evidence. “Join us” without a bounded action and named return. “Impact” when the page means activity or intention. “The community” as a substitute for a responsible owner. “AI-powered” as a claim of wisdom. Dramatic statistics without provenance. Smooth paragraphs that erase disagreement, failure or the people affected.

What readers need to know

  1. Why does this matter now?
  2. What is true today?
  3. What is the open question?
  4. What can I do that is useful?
  5. How will I know what happened afterward?

The homepage is a live workbench, not an institutional brochure. Its primary object is the current challenge, surrounded by status, evidence, contribution and receipt.

Visual language

The visual system comes from the station and the harbor: white shingle, red roof, sea at the mouth of the Piscataqua, brass fittings, printed ledgers and the geometry of a life ring.

Palette

#F4F1E9 paper · #10232A water · #0B1A1F night watch · #D9AE5F brass light · #C4402B action · #2E6B78 measured water.

Type

Newsreader for what deserves to be remembered. Public Sans for what must be understood. IBM Plex Mono for what can be counted, dated or checked.

Use flat fields, strong type, quiet borders and real photographs. Avoid gradients, stock-future imagery, dashboard gloss, decorative animation and badges that imply authority.

When we ask for help

The intended response is voluntary agency, not conversion. A page may ask someone to inspect a claim, bring a local constraint, write a pilot or challenge a premise. It must say how long it takes, who benefits, where the work lands, who reviews it and what comes back.

Measure useful response: challenge inspected, first contribution completed, response time, review completed, correction adopted, pilot run and next decision improved. Do not optimize for raw signups, session length, reactions, streaks or moral pressure.

Before publishing

Could a skeptical practitioner tell what exists in twenty seconds?
Have we explained why this matters without frightening people into acting?
Is the gap between aspiration and evidence visible?
Is there one useful move for ten minutes?
Does the page preserve choice, privacy, dissent and the right to leave?
