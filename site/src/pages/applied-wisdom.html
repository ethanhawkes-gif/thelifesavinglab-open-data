Applied Wisdom · proposed operating contract

Wisdom is applied when it informs a real decision.

A conversation, insight, model output, citation or training session can be valuable without being applied wisdom. The first observable test is whether it informed a real decision: what someone chose to do, not do, prioritize, preserve, escalate, defer or stop. Sometimes that means changing course; sometimes it means reaffirming or preserving the current course for good reasons.

A changed decision—or a current plan kept after checking the facts—shows that learning was used, not that it helped anyone yet. The Lab keeps each step separate: a decision is not action, action is not adoption, adoption is not proven benefit, and benefit is not a life saved.

The receipt

Every consequential learning loop should preserve, at the smallest responsible level:

Situation: whose decision, where, when and under what conditions?
Question: what decision was actually being made?
Prior path: what would probably have happened without the intervention?
Evidence and dissent: what was learned, challenged or left uncertain?
Decision judgment: what changed, was reaffirmed, preserved, deferred, escalated or stopped, and why?
Authority: who decided, who could act and who bore consequences?
Action: what was done, deferred, stopped or handed off?
Expected and observed result: what was anticipated and what happened?
Review: who checked the interpretation, with what limits?
Next move: adopt, adapt, repeat, stop or investigate—and who owns it?

“The decision did not change,” “nobody with authority approved the action,” and “the result could not be measured” are useful, honest findings—not writing failures to hide.

Human and AI roles

AI may assemble evidence, identify contradictions, preserve memory, explore alternatives and draft decision records. It must state what it read, what it did not read and what remains uncertain. Humans retain responsibility for values, consent, local judgment, safety, authority, adoption, publication, deployment and repair.

Red lines

No applied-wisdom claim without a named decision, its prior path, and the reasoning for changing, reaffirming, preserving, deferring, escalating or stopping it. No action represented as authorized merely because an agent recommended it. No positive outcome presented as verified benefit without appropriate review. No emergency guidance, rescue function or “lives saved” claim follows from a prototype or decision receipt alone. Dissent, refusal, reversal and stop decisions belong in the record.
