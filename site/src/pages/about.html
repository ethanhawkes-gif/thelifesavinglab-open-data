About the Lab

A practical place to learn good judgment.

Wood Island has a restored lifesaving station and a surfboat that still runs on its marine railway. The Lab asks a present-day question: what useful work could students do for the people helping others on the water?

Applied Wisdom provides the learning framework. Wood Island provides the setting, local knowledge and community connections. Students and experts bring the questions; AI assistants help with research, drafts and review. People remain responsible for checking the work and making decisions.

There is an old surfboat in this story, and some very new tools. Neither is a substitute for knowing what you are doing.
