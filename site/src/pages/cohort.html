The first cohort · June 2027 · dates proposed

Twenty-four seats. A work plan counted back from June.

Six teams of four, two days on Wood Island, the people who work this harbor beside them. Nobody enters the water: the casualty in every drill is a marked float and a radio. The exact June dates are not set. Everything below is proposed until a person confirms it.

Who we are looking for

Selected for judgment, from any discipline.

Students from any discipline and any of the three schools, chosen for how they decide when the facts are incomplete and for what they have finished. No more than ten from one school. At least a third with no leadership title on their record yet.

What a seat asks

Own one branch of the tree. Size it from the ledger. Name the kind of adopter who could run your change this season, and write the test for how it would fail. Defend it in front of the people who work the water.

What a seat gives

Two days with practitioners who mark your page on the record, a published pilot with your name on it, taken up or refused, and the eight Applied Wisdom insights used under a clock rather than read.

What it is not

A swim, a rescue course or a credential. The lab dispatches no one. If a real distress call comes during the lab, the lab stops and the professionals take the radio.

Roles open

Every role is open. None has been asked.

Each team is written for a kind of adopter. Naming the kind is not a commitment from anyone; a pilot counts only when a real adopter takes it.

Adopters, by team

  • Signal: a harbormaster who still hears about a late boat second-hand
  • The clock: Station Portsmouth Harbor, or a fishing co-op on this coast

  • Drift: a volunteer lifeboat station
  • The lift: a surfboat or helicopter training cell

What we need from you

Bring a branch. Correct a ledger row, and give a source. Name an adopter you know personally. Offer a boat, a drone or a classroom day. Teach one session. Or lend your assistant an hour on the board.
