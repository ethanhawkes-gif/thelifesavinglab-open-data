The compact

A person and a model, in that order.

This is a method, not a membership. The cause is deaths in and on the water: sea, coast, river, pond, pool, bath, and the spans above them. A lifeguard and a late-boat watch are the same cause; they are not the same count. Nothing here adds a pool to a fishing fleet. These eight rules are how the lab works with people and with models, and every page on this site is meant to keep them.

The eight rules

  1. 01

    People decide

    A model may propose. A proposal no person has edited is not a proposal. The edit is the collaboration, and it stays visible.

  2. 02

    Nothing here dispatches

    Distress is VHF channel 16, or the Coast Guard. A draft, a counter or a branch of the tree does not start a boat and does not tell anyone to wait.

  3. 03

    The disagreement stays

    24,000 and 100,000 are not averaged into a number that feels finished. Where the sources disagree, the ledger shows both.

  4. 04

    The dead are not a metric

    A range is allowed. An invented name, an invented toll or an invented rescue is not. If it is not in the ledger, say so.

  5. 05

    A change needs an adopter

    The work is real when a named service could take it up this year without a new agency. A movement that only produces a movement has not saved anyone.

  6. 06

    Open, not silently writable

    Anyone may propose. No one may overwrite the record from a browser. You export the text; a person merges it. That is how an open ledger stays safe enough to use.

  7. 07

    The branch, not the person

    An idea is judged only by what it does to a branch: the gap it names, the change it asks, the service that could adopt it, and how you would know it failed. Office, fame, fluency, and whether a person or a model said it are not inputs. People overweight whoever spoke last and whoever holds rank; a model overweights whatever it is rewarded for saying. Both are taken out of the judgment.

  8. 08

    Credit for bringing

    Name the person for bringing the idea, including the part that was wrong. Do not score them, and do not rank them against a model. A model can draft; it cannot be the author. A correction is kept on the same terms as a measure: if the ledger gets truer or a branch gets a named adopter. Being corrected in public has to cost less than staying silent, or the next person keeps the bad number.
