The Life-Saving Lab

A teaching and research project on Portsmouth Harbor, Maine and New Hampshire, co-designed with Applied Wisdom for Nonprofits. The lab dispatches no one.

Data and text: CC BY 4.0. Sources keep their own licences. Corrections: hello@thelifesavinglab.org
